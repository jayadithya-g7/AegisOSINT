### Research Gap Addressed
Current research suffers from **five fatal structural gaps**:
1. **Multimodal Siloing**: Domain tools ignore social media; vision models ignore DNS/ASN infrastructure; social monitors ignore phishing domains.
2. **False Positives in Standalone Classifiers**: Pure visual matching fails on geometric shapes; pure lexical matching flags benign fan sites.
3. **Missing Infrastructure Linkage**: Treating attacks as isolated events fails to unmask the shared hosting ASNs, nameservers, and crypto drainer wallets behind organized syndicates.
4. **Absence of Chain-of-Custody**: Zero cryptographic hashing or timestamping is used, causing evidence to be rejected in ICANN UDRP disputes and courtroom proceedings.
5. **Remediation Disconnect**: Systems detect threats but fail to generate formal ICANN Section 3.18 abuse notices, hosting cease-and-desist letters, or STIX 2.1 threat packages.

## 🔍 What Was Missing in Classic Research Papers (Identified via OSINT Framework & Elicit)

While classical papers (*Kintis et al., Acharya et al., Liu et al., Abdelnabi et al., Oest et al.*) addressed basic typosquatting, single-platform social media monitoring, and visual CNN logo matching, they suffered from **six major blind spots**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│             TACTICAL CAPABILITIES ABSENT FROM CLASSIC LITERATURE PAPERS               │
├──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Pre-DNS CT Log Feeds  │ 2. Temporal Web Archives │ 3. Cross-Platform Footprinting   │

├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Burner Email OSINT    │ 5. Anti-Cloaking & C2    │ 6. Explainable AI (XAI) Matrix   │
│ Holehe/MX analysis flags │ Unmasks bot-blocking and │ Transparent SHAP/LIME breakdown  │
│ disposable domains &     │ form exfiltration to     │ replacing black-box scalars with │
│ spearphishing patterns.  │ Telegram Bot/Discord C2. │ court-defensible factor weights. │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Pre-DNS Certificate Transparency (CT) Stream Reconnaissance (`crt.sh`)**:
   - *The Gap*: Classic papers only analyzed active websites *after* DNS propagation and lure transmission. As demonstrated by Oest et al. (*PhishFarm*), automated crawlers suffer an 18–24 hour detection delay.
   - *Our Extension*: Leverages append-only CT logs (`crt.sh`) to detect SSL/TLS certificates issued for brand subdomains (`login-brand.portal.top`) at the moment of Certificate Authority issuance, enabling **pre-operational discovery**.
2. **Historical Web Archive Forensics (`Wayback Machine CDX API`)**:
   - *The Gap*: Prior research treated domain age as a static WHOIS creation date, failing to detect **domain drop-catching** (where threat actors buy expired aged domains with established reputation and deploy phishing kits).
   - *Our Extension*: Uses the Internet Archive CDX API to inspect historical digest checksums and detect sudden semantic shifts or zero-history staging.
3. **Cross-Platform Actor Footprinting (*Sherlock / WhatsMyName Style*)**:
   - *The Gap*: Literature evaluated individual social networks in isolation (e.g. Acharya et al. focused primarily on Twitter).
   - *Our Extension*: Probes 9+ platforms simultaneously (Twitter/X, Telegram, GitHub, Reddit, Instagram, TikTok, Medium, Pinterest, Steam) to map the threat actor's identity constellation.
4. **Scammer Email & Disposable Burner Domain Triage (*Holehe / MX OSINT*)**:
   - *The Gap*: Classic models focused on website DOMs, treating sender email addresses as arbitrary strings.
   - *Our Extension*: Implements disposable burner email triage (Mailinator, GuerrillaMail, Yopmail) and analyzes deceptive local-part generation patterns.
5. **Anti-Cloaking & Form Action C2 Exfiltration Detection (*Brand Domain Identification 2025/2026*)**:
   - *The Gap*: Traditional visual models assumed the target site displays uniformly to all crawlers. Modern evasive kits actively deploy anti-bot defenses (disabling DevTools/right-click, user-agent filtering, Cloudflare Turnstile).
   - *Our Extension*: Evaluates `<form action="...">` attributes to identify **off-origin C2 exfiltration channels** (direct POSTs to Telegram Bot APIs `api.telegram.org/bot...` and Discord Webhooks) regardless of visual cloaking.
6. **Explainable AI (XAI) & SHAP/LIME Transparent Attribution**:
   - *The Gap*: Deep Siamese or Triplet networks output opaque similarity scores, which fail analyst scrutiny in courtroom proceedings or registrar UDRP disputes.
   - *Our Extension*: Implements transparent feature factor attribution, decomposing composite risk into mathematically bounded, human-interpretable vectors.
